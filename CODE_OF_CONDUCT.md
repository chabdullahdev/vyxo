# Code of Conduct

VYXO is a small project run by volunteers. We want it to be a place where
anyone can ask a question, report a bug, or send a fix without being treated
badly.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with maintainers, who are often busy.
- Don't harass, demean, or discriminate against anyone, and don't share other
  people's private information.

## What happens if someone doesn't

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who
break these rules. To report a problem, email hello@mabdullahshahzad.com. Reports
are handled privately.

This is a short plain-language policy. It is inspired by the Contributor
Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
